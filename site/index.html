What you can do

  • Trade with real live prices from Binance, Finnhub and Yahoo Finance, including NSE shares
  • Market, Limit, Stop-Loss and Take-Profit orders with realistic spread and slippage
  • Candlestick, line and Heikin-Ashi charts with SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD and VWAP
  • Backtest strategies on a year of real daily candles
  • Run auto-trading bots that follow a strategy or your own price rule
  • Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, win rate and profit factor
  • Trade history analytics with a capital gains tax estimate
  • Leaderboard, multiple users and a rotatable 3D portfolio chart
